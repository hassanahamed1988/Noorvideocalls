# v2.1 – Calls Me and status

- **Calls Me OFF** for a user: that user no longer sees your "Online" status. They see "Last seen" with the date and time frozen at the moment you turned Calls Me off, even if Last Seen & Online Status is ON. It stays frozen (also when you come online later) until you turn Calls Me ON again, then the status is live again.
- Last Seen & Online Status OFF still hides everything (it comes first).
- The other phone updates instantly when you switch Calls Me.

Files: server/index.js only.
