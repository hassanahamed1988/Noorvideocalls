# v2.2 – Screenshot blocks, Who Can See My Profile, Voice Messages

- **Block Screenshot For Calls** (switch ON = blocked): during a video/audio call the OTHER person cannot take screenshots / record the screen on their phone. The other person's switch does the same to you. (Your own switch no longer locks your own screen.) The per-chat call screenshot setting in Contact Info is unchanged.
- **Block Screenshot for Profile** (switch ON = blocked): unchanged, works for anyone opening your profile.
- **Lock Profile** switch removed.
- **Who Can See My Profile**: Everyone / My Contacts / Nobody. My Contacts opens a list of your contacts: only the ones you tick see your profile details. Tap the setting again and choose "Select contacts" to change the list. Accounts that were on My Contacts before keep "all contacts" until you pick a list. Bio, cover and user ID are no longer shown to people who may not see your profile (contact search, Contact Info).
- **Voice Messages** (new, Settings > Privacy): "Who Can Share My Voice Messages": Everyone / My Contacts (pick contacts) / Nobody. The Share button now appears on received voice messages when the sender allows you; view-once voice messages cannot be shared.

Files: server/index.js, app/lib/main.dart, app/lib/contact_strings.dart.
